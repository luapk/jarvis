# POV HUD shot: suit configuration on a street

An image or video generation prompt for a pitch mood shot. Deliberately uses
generic powered-suit archetypes and no character names, in keeping with the
build's clearance-free stance.

## Prompt

First-person POV looking out through the inside of a sleek armoured helmet
visor onto a busy city street at dusk. The visor glass carries a dense,
elegant heads-up display in glowing cyan with warm orange accents: a large
central targeting reticle, concentric radar rings, telemetry readouts,
integrity bars, and small rotating gauges around the edges. Floating in the
mid-air ahead, several translucent holographic wireframe renders of different
powered exo-suits and hero costume archetypes rotate slowly side by side, as
if being configured and compared: a heavy industrial powered-armour rig, a
sleek stealth tactical suit, a flowing caped monarch's armour, a lithe
acrobatic bodysuit with a fine surface lattice, a bulky brute-strength frame.
Each wireframe is mid-scan, with schematic call-out lines, part labels, load
percentages, and "CONFIGURING" and "SCAN" status text ticking beside it.
Ordinary pedestrians and traffic move on the street beyond the glass, softly
out of focus. Cinematic, high contrast, volumetric light, shallow depth of
field, faint chromatic aberration and scan-line texture on the visor,
photoreal with a premium sci-fi finish. Colour palette: near-black, electric
cyan, and burnt orange.

## Notes

- No trademarked names, logos, or specific characters: the suits are generic
  archetypes only. Keep it that way for anything shown outside an internal
  pitch.
- Aspect ratio: 21:9 or 16:9 for a hero frame; 9:16 if you need a vertical cut.
- For a moving version, animate the wireframes rotating and the HUD telemetry
  updating, with the street parallaxing gently behind the glass.
